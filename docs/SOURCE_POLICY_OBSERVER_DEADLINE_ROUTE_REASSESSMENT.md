## Why stop this detour instead of tuning another guard

The parent requirement is genuine minimum PHP/core/native source-policy
qualification before trusted connection/form integration, not a product feature
that inventories PowerShell. A full metadata snapshot would still leave the
existing host-admission tool-DATA cap of 128, exact file-hash/path contracts,
explicit platform/CRT trust and candidate loader preflight unresolved. A catalog
digest is not a DLL-content or signature/provenance manifest. Resolving the
deadline alone therefore would not close the parent gate.

The historical [host-trust/observer design](SOURCE_POLICY_MINIMUM_HOST_TRUST_OBSERVER_DESIGN.md)
expressly avoids a custom loader, recursive host trust verifier or protected
staging machinery. Its existing-host trust proposal is not actual consent and
has not become a runtime grant. Further instrumentation, broader snapshots or
relaxed timing would be additional scoped work with uncertain value; they are
not selected now. Do not reopen the compiler/account/privilege route or silently
replace fail-closed observation with trust in a path/hash claim.

Retain the failed route as **deferred-unqualified**, not succeeded, unnecessary,
deleted or universally impossible. Reopening it requires a new explicit decision
with concrete expected benefit and bounded evidence, not another "Proceed" or
routine green CI. This does not defer the genuine minimum qualification requirement
or reopen unrelated deferred features. Earlier current-source Windows/TCP and
Linux results remain valid only within their own recorded source/profile scope.
