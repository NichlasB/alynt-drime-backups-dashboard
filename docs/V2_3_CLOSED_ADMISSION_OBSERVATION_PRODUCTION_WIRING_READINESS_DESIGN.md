## Selected same-root continuation contract

This refines the parent lifecycle design; it does not replace its closed policy.

1. One fixed dormant lifecycle root starts its monotonic/UTC bounds before initial
   capture and consumes its entry latch even on refusal/reentry. It owns the
   genuine request, original installation/blog, actor/cookie/session, enrollment
   context, original mutation command, minimal origin and complete used/reserved
   identities. Retention occurs before the first possible DML. No caller-supplied
   origin, imported owner, handle, closure, certificate or public continuation.
2. Admission may attempt only its existing closed ledger registration. Preserve
   actual raw acknowledgements, source identities and refusal behavior; false is
   never promoted to committed or current. A native qualification result remains
   unchanged diagnostic DATA. Observe only an eligible committed or uncertain
   history whose actual client-owned resources were released, not not-written,
   acknowledged rolled-back, unsupported, malformed or release-unknown history.
   This is only a prerequisite to fresh diagnostic admission, not authority.
3. Preserve only own minimal primitive origin/identity set and original clock for
   that one continuation. Full serialized frames, proposals, nonces, writer seals
   and write-capable handles must be released before read authorization callbacks.
   Do not retain the original full qualification pin across callbacks. An internal
   handoff must be fixed and consumed, never a public setter for another owner's
   capture latch. No reflection or proof harness is a runtime permission boundary.
4. After actual release, independently revalidate current genuine local actor,
   cookie/session, capability, installation/blog, enrollment/context, physical
   borrowed authentication connection and original POST/mutation intent under
   the selected same-request diagnostic policy. A missing post-admission guard in
   the original false path is not implicitly true and cannot be borrowed from a
   prior proof. No protected read or fresh lookup UUID before this authorization.
5. Construct/verify the separate small read nonce bound to the retained original
   command, then reserve a different fresh read-command identity and construct/
   verify the full read nonce. Preserve both domains and collision checks against
   the complete retained set. Renew read guards at handoff claim, before native
   BEGIN and after acknowledged barrier close, with no WordPress callback while
   locking storage. Nonces constrain intent, not durable permission or role locks.
6. Original admission remains capped at10s; diagnostic observation at10s within
   the original20s root cap and unexpired UTC intent. A new read start cannot reset
   root age, revive expired intent or enlarge any native timeout/resource limit.
   These are acceptance limits, not guaranteed cancellation of blocking I/O.
7. At most one fresh diagnostic observation, no second write, resend, renewal or
   retry. Real barrier must use its own exclusively owned session and fixed
   metadata/lock order/zero-DML checks. Client release does not certify that a
   server transaction disappeared; bounded lock refusal remains unavailable.
8. A historical matching closed receipt is not permission to execute. A verified
   absence at the locking barrier is not proof a paused/nonparticipating writer
   can never act later. No closure_proven, retry_supported, storage_supported,
   dispatch_supported or execution_supported promotion. No raw origin/nonce/
   command/SQL/session data in public diagnostics or a durable resume record.
9. Failure, callback exception, invalidation, timeout, reentry or return consumes
   and erases all private lifecycle slots in finally. Loss of the request requires
   manual inspection; no reconstruction from a result, new login, transient,
   option, command receipt or synthetic transcript. Never make a fail-closed owner
   succeed by adding an artificial success path.

A future dormant implementation must have a new explicit source identity and
proof owner. It must not silently alter the previously pinned admission/worker
sources while citing old hashes or old native results as proof of the new join.
